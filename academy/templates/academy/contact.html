Before you send it

Enquiry FAQ

Quick answers to what usually comes up right before someone reaches out.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. There's no charge and no commitment for the initial call. It's just to understand your goals and figure out the right tutor and plan.

How soon will I hear back?

We usually respond within one business day. WhatsApp tends to be the fastest way to reach us if it's urgent.

How is a tutor assigned to me?

After the consultation, we match you with a tutor based on the subject, your current level, and your preferred schedule and format.

Can I switch tutors if it's not a good fit?

Yes, just let us know and we'll arrange a different tutor. Fit matters more to us than sticking with a first match that isn't working.

Do I need to decide on a course before contacting you?

No. If you're not sure yet, mention that in the form or on the call and we'll help you figure out the right fit.
