Our mission

Every learner deserves a tutor, not just a timetable.

Classroom teaching moves at one speed for thirty students. We built SureLearn Academy around a simpler idea: learning sticks when the pace, the method and the attention are built around one person.

That's why every lesson is one-on-one. Whether it's a student cramming for WAEC or a career-switcher learning to code, the tutor adjusts to how that person actually learns, not the other way round.

We work across two worlds, exam prep and tech skills, because both come down to the same thing: a clear plan, a tutor who's actually paying attention, and consistent practice over time.

Founded by Michael Israel Eneche

SureLearn Academy started with one tutor and a handful of students in Abuja, and grew on the same principle it started with: teach the person, not the class.

How we teach

Our approach, in four parts

The same model runs through every course we offer, from SSCE prep to AI & Machine Learning.

01

One tutor, one plan

No fixed curriculum applied to everyone. Each learner gets a plan shaped around their current level, goals and timeline.

02

Free consultation first

Before any commitment, we talk through what the learner needs and match them with the right tutor for it, not just the nearest available one.

03

Home, online, or both

Lessons happen wherever they work best: in person across Abuja and nearby Nasarawa areas (Mararaba, Abacha Road, New Nyanya & Masaka), online from anywhere, or a mix depending on the week.

04

Progress that's visible

Regular check-ins with parents and learners so everyone can see what's improving and what still needs work, with no guessing until exam day.

What we care about

Relevance

Lessons built around real exams and real tools, not generic material that doesn't map to what's ahead.
